# R2: multi-round compaction of F on Qwen3.8-27B (full run)

Preregistered in `PREREG.md`, section "Rung R2", with Amendments 1 and 2 (Amendment 2, commit a8bb20e2, was written before any full-run row). Run 2026-10-14 on one Modal H200, `r2.py::pilot --probes 0..19 --ks 1,8 --arms FULL,F-SEG,F-BLK --out r2_full`, wall 2952 s, $3.72. Selection split, loader indices 0-19, 357 questions. k is the number of distractor articles in front of the questioned article; k = 0 rows are the R1b single-round rows (`orig`, `F`) from the H100/H200 mix.

Arms (all at 0.10):
- FULL: no compaction.
- F-SEG: each article compacted once with per-head budgets when it arrives, never recompacted.
- F-BLK: every 640-token block compacted once to 64 slots with per-head budgets, the earlier blocks as prefix.

Context at k = 8: 50,024 tokens on average; F-SEG keeps 5,054 effective slots, F-BLK 5,270.

## Preregistered question 1 (primary)

F-SEG(8) - F(0) = **-0.6 points [-3.3, +2.2]**, McNemar 12 / 14, p = 0.845. The rule asks for a lower bound of at least -3. At -3.3 it is not met, so the answer is **inconclusive at n = 20 articles**: the point estimate shows almost no loss from compacting eight more articles, but this sample cannot prove the loss stays under 3 points.

Next to it: FULL(8) - F-SEG(8) = +2.2 [-1.1, +5.4]. At the same 50K context, compaction is about 2 points behind full KV.

## F-BLK (exploratory)

- F-BLK(8) - F(0) = -3.6 [-8.0, +0.6], McNemar 12 / 25, p = 0.047. F-BLK(1) - F(0) = -2.8 [-5.6, +0.0].
- FULL - F-BLK: +6.7 [+4.2, +9.2] at k = 1, +5.3 [+1.1, +9.5] at k = 8.
- Most of the loss is already there at k = 1. Going from 1 to 8 distractors costs 3 more questions, so the loss does not compound with more rounds.
- These arms use per-head budgets on a 64-slot block, where 12 heads get 2 slots. R1(e) used uniform budgets at the same block size and lost nothing against whole-article F (+0.74 [-1.87, +3.36]). This fits the engine's choice of uniform budgets.
